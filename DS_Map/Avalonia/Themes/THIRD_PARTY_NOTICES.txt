The control themes and colour dictionaries in this folder (Controls.axaml,
ControlColours.axaml, Colours/SoftDark.axaml, and ControlStyles/*.axaml) are
vendored and adapted from the Avalonia theme in WPFDarkTheme.

    Project: WPFDarkTheme  (Theme.Avalonia)
    Source:  https://github.com/AngryCarrot789/WPFDarkTheme
    License: MIT

Local adaptations:
  - ControlStyles/Window.axaml and the custom WindowEx / GroupBox controls and
    the GroupBoxGapToThicknessConverter were omitted so this is pure XAML.
  - ControlStyles/GroupBox.axaml was rewritten to target Avalonia's built-in
    GroupBox instead of the theme's custom GroupBox control.
